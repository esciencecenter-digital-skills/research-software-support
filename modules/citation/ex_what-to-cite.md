## Exercise: To cite, or not to cite, that is the question...
Suppose you are writing a paper on your latest research and are putting together the reference list. During your research you have used all the software mentioned below.

1. _Ubuntu_ - operating system
1. _Microsoft Word_ - text editor (used to write the paper, take notes)
1. _Git_ - version control software
1. _NumPy_ - Python math library (used in nearly all Python-based research software)
1. _ESMValTool_ - diagnostic tool/library for Earth System Models (Used in many climate research projects)
1. _ClimAn_ - your own domain specific Python library (defines various analytical functions that you used in your climate research)
1. The original code on which you based your library _ClimAn_
1. Your own local Python script, used to execute the analysis

Which software would you cite and which not? Why?
