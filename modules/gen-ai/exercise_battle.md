## Exercise 1: Compare Different LLM Models (15 mins)

- Discuss with two generative AI models at the same time, use <a href="https://arena.ai">arena.ai</a> in **battle mode** — two AIs answer the same prompt, you vote for the better response.
- Indicate after each round which one is better
  - If one "wins" a round, it's answers will be used as context for the follow-up questions for *both* bodels
  - Be aware that they will be *very* confident in their approaches. Make sure to understand and judge every aspect of the discussion.

<div style="text-align:left; margin-top:1rem;">
  <p><strong>Round 1 — "Easy"</strong></p>
  <blockquote style="margin:0.3rem 0 1rem 1rem; border-left:3px solid var(--color-border-concept); padding-left:0.8rem; color:var(--color-prose);">
    I plan to wash my car. The car wash is 50m away. Should I take the car or go by foot?
  </blockquote>
  <p><strong>Round 2 — "Complex"</strong></p>
  <blockquote style="margin:0.3rem 0 1rem 1rem; border-left:3px solid var(--color-border-accent); padding-left:0.8rem; color:var(--color-prose);">
    <p>I have 10GB of spectral measurement data I'd like to analyze. Which programming language should I use, and why?</p>
    <p>This is intentionally incomplete — iterate, add constraints, push for a concrete architecture.</p>
  </blockquote>
  <p><strong>Reflection</strong> — What are the AIs doing well? Where do they struggle?</p>
</div>
