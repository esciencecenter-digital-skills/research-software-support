In this module you will learn to answer the following questions:

- Explain where Gen AI fits in the wider AI landscape
- Compare models, products and deployment options
- Advise on privacy, security, bias, reliability and research integrity
- Connect AI use to reproducible and sustainable research practice
- Help researchers document and declare AI use clearly
- Which questions can we not answer yet?
