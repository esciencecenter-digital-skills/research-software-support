## Exercise 2: Client Settings (15 mins)

Choose ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude or another available system.

- Check the settings
  - Is your data used for training new models?
- Find which model you are using
  - See the differences between lighter and heavier models
  - Look up information about the models, what are their strengthes and weaknesses?
- Find documentation of how the vendor applies privacy & security (GDPR)
- Does the vendor have a signed DPA (Data Processing Addendum)?

Read up about ZDR and DPA at https://aiprivacy.pro/guides/enterprise-ai-dpa-vs-zdr .
