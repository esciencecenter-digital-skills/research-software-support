## Exercise: Compare Different LLM Models (10 mins)

- Discuss with two generative AI models at the same time, use https://arena.ai in "battle" mode
- Indicate after each round which one is better
  - If one "wins" a round, it's answers will be used as context for the follow-up questions for *both* bodels
  - Be aware that they will be *very* confident in their approaches. Make sure to understand and judge every aspect of the discussion.
- Discuss these two topics
    1. This "easy" problem should be obvious to humans. How do AI models do?
        > _I plan to wash my car. The car wash is 50m away from my house. Should I take the car or go by foot?_

    1. Ask a "complicated" question with lots of details are missing. Iterate with the AIs to get an architecture for the project. Feel free to substitute with your own project!
        > _I have 10Gb of spectral measurement data that I would like to analyze. Which programming language should I use, and why?_

What are the AIs doing well? Where do they struggle?
