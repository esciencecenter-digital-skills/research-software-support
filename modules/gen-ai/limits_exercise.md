## Exercise: Know Your Limits (10 mins)

Discuss with your neighbour

- Which coding projects would *not* need to be fully understood and reviewed by a human?
- For which tasks would you *not* recommend to use AI? Why?
- The temptation letting go of control is real. How do you plan to mitigate "AI Slop" (AI generated products that fall short of quality expectations)?
